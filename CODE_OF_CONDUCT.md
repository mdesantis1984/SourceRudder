# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

SourceRudder is built for rigorous technical collaboration. Everyone who
participates is expected to make that collaboration safe, direct, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and explain the technical reasoning behind it.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and access needs.
- Protect private reports, credentials, personal data, and embargoed findings.
- Correct mistakes openly and give credit for other people's work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository activity, project communication, events, and
other spaces where someone represents SourceRudder.

## Reporting

Do not report sensitive conduct incidents in an issue. Contact a maintainer
through the private collaboration channel used to grant repository access and
prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. This confidential route is separate from the
technical vulnerability process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will acknowledge the report privately, limit access to people who
need it, examine available evidence, and communicate the resulting action when
it is safe to do so. Responses may include a warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, permanent exclusion, or referral to GitHub.

No one may retaliate against a person who raises a good-faith concern.
